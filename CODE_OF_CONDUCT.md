# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and harassment-free project community. Participants should assume good faith, critique technical work rather than people, protect sensitive information, and respect legal authorization boundaries.

## Expected behavior

- Be accurate about evidence, scope, and uncertainty.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive review.
- Disclose conflicts that could affect interpretation of results.
- Protect credentials, personal data, and unpublished vulnerability information.
- Do not pressure others to perform unsafe or unauthorized testing.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of secrets, deliberate misrepresentation of research, unauthorized access, or using project channels to coordinate harmful activity is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `support@pcnaid.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and community.
